Library Management System

The Library Management System is a web-based system designed to make library services more convenient, organized, and accessible for Senior High School students.

Through this system, students can view available books, search for books, borrow books, and keep track of their borrowed books.

Browse Books

Easily view and search for books available in the school library.

Borrow Books

Borrow available books by providing the required information.

Track Books

Keep track of borrowed books and return them when needed.
